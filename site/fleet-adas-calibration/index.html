What to include

Better details create faster follow-up.

For fleet, dealership, and shop requests, the most useful information is business name, contact person, phone, email, vehicle count, VINs or vehicle descriptions, location, timing needs, and whether the vehicle may have ADAS features.

  • Business name and contact person
  • Vehicle year, make, model, VIN, or inventory details
  • Glass type, damage description, and ADAS features if known
  • Preferred location, timing, and recurring work needs

Questions

Fleet ADAS calibration FAQ.

Can Shoreline support dealerships and body shops?

Yes. Dealerships, used car lots, body shops, and service departments can request auto glass and ADAS support through the fleet request form.

Can multiple vehicles be included in one request?

Yes. Include vehicle count, VINs or descriptions, glass needs, location, and timing so Shoreline can follow up with a useful next step.

Do you support ADAS calibration for commercial vehicles?

Shoreline reviews ADAS-related needs as part of commercial windshield replacement and fleet auto glass requests. Support depends on the vehicle and required procedure.
