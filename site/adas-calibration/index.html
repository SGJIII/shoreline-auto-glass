Questions

ADAS calibration FAQ.

Do all windshield replacements need ADAS calibration?

No. Calibration depends on the vehicle, safety features, glass type, and manufacturer procedure. Share your VIN so Shoreline can review the request.

What vehicle features may be connected to ADAS calibration?

Features can include lane departure warnings, forward collision alerts, adaptive cruise control, traffic sign recognition, and automatic emergency braking.

Can dealerships and fleets request ADAS support?

Yes. Dealerships, body shops, service departments, and fleet operators can request recurring or multi-vehicle support through the fleet page.
