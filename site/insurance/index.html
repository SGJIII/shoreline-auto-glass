Questions

Auto glass insurance FAQ.

Can I choose Shoreline Auto Glass instead of the shop suggested by my carrier?

Yes, you can ask for Shoreline by name. Your carrier may discuss network vendors, but you can request the shop you want for your auto glass claim.

Will insurance cover my windshield replacement?

Coverage depends on your policy, deductible, damage, and carrier approval. Comprehensive coverage often applies to glass claims, but your carrier or agent should confirm your specific policy.

Can insurance cover ADAS recalibration?

Many carriers review recalibration as part of a covered windshield replacement when required for the vehicle. Coverage depends on your policy and carrier approval.

What if the claim already started with another glass company?

Call your carrier or claims administrator and say you want to use Shoreline Auto Glass instead. Then call Shoreline so we can help confirm the next step.
