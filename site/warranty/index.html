Lifetime Workmanship Warranty

Last updated: June 3, 2026

At Shoreline Auto Glass, we stand behind the quality of our work. We take pride in providing safe, professional auto glass installation and service.

Lifetime Workmanship Warranty

Shoreline Auto Glass provides a lifetime workmanship warranty on all auto glass installations performed by our company. This warranty remains in effect for as long as the original customer owns the vehicle.

This warranty is not transferable and does not apply to any future owners of the vehicle.

What Is Covered

Our lifetime workmanship warranty covers installation-related issues caused by workmanship, including but not limited to:

  • Water leaks related to the installation.
  • Air leaks or wind noise related to the installation.
  • Improper seal or bonding related to the installation.
  • Other workmanship-related installation concerns.

If an issue is determined to be related to the workmanship of the installation performed by Shoreline Auto Glass, we will inspect the vehicle and correct the issue at no charge to the customer.

What Is Not Covered

This warranty does not cover damage to the glass after installation, including but not limited to:

  • Rock chips.
  • Cracks.
  • Impact damage.
  • Stress cracks not related to installation workmanship.
  • Vandalism.
  • Collision damage.
  • Damage caused by improper use, neglect, or unrelated vehicle issues.

Damage to the glass after installation is the responsibility of the vehicle owner and is not covered under this workmanship warranty.

Warranty Limitations

This warranty applies only to workmanship performed by Shoreline Auto Glass. It does not cover issues caused by:

  • Pre-existing rust, corrosion, body damage, or structural issues.
  • Prior improper repairs or installations by another company.
  • Vehicle modifications.
  • Accidents, impacts, or environmental damage.
  • Failure to follow safe drive-away time or aftercare instructions.

Warranty Claims

Customers who believe they have a workmanship-related issue should contact Shoreline Auto Glass to schedule an inspection. Shoreline Auto Glass reserves the right to inspect the vehicle and determine whether the issue is covered under this warranty.
