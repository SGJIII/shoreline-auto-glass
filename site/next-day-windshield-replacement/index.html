What affects timing

Fast service starts with the right information.

VIN and Glass Match

Your VIN helps us identify the correct windshield, sensors, tint, moldings, and other vehicle-specific options.

02

Next-day timing depends on whether the exact glass and required installation materials are available from the distributor.

Questions

Fast windshield replacement FAQ.

Can Shoreline replace my windshield tomorrow?

Often, yes. Next-day appointments may be available when the correct glass, technician schedule, weather, location, and any calibration requirements align. Submit your VIN and damage details so we can confirm.

Do you guarantee same-day windshield replacement?

No. We will always tell you the earliest safe appointment available, but same-day service cannot be guaranteed. Glass availability and vehicle-specific requirements come first.

Is mobile windshield replacement available?

Yes. Shoreline provides mobile service at homes, workplaces, dealerships, body shops, fleet locations, and other suitable appointment locations.

Can next-day service include ADAS calibration?

It may, depending on the vehicle and required calibration procedure. We review ADAS needs before confirming timing.

How fast can you reach Martha's Vineyard or Nantucket?

Island timing depends on glass availability, ferry access, service routing, weather, and the vehicle's requirements. Send the location and VIN so Shoreline can provide the earliest available island service date.
